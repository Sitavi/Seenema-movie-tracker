Track the films and series you have watched, rate them out of five, and keep a list of what to watch next.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Features:

* Search films, series and people from a single box
* Rate anything in one tap, which files it under Seen at the same time
* Keep a Watch later list, and titles move over once you rate them
* Title pages with the synopsis, IMDb score, cast, full crew and the trailer, which plays in the app
* People pages with a complete filmography
* Add a personal note and the date you watched something
* Your list is a plain CSV file on your phone that you can export and import back

Titles, posters and synopses come from IMDb in your phone's language wherever they exist.
