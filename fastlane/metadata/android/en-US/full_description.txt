Track the films and series you have watched, rate them out of five, and keep a list of what to watch next.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Features:

🔍 <b>Search</b> films, series and people.
⭐ <b>Rate in one tap</b> a film or a series to add it to your seen list.
🕐 <b>Watch later</b> saves anything for later, and it moves over to your seen list once you rate it.
🎞️ <b>Title pages</b> with the synopsis, IMDb score, cast, complete crew, and trailer, which plays in the app.
👤 <b>People pages</b> with their full filmography.
📝 <b>Notes</b> field to add a personal note and the date you watched something.
📄 <b>Backup</b> is easy with a plain CSV file on your phone, not an account somewhere.

Titles, posters and synopses come from IMDb in your phone's language wherever they exist.
